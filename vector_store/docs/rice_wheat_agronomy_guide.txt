RICE AND WHEAT CROP MANAGEMENT & CULTIVATION ADVISORY
Source Document: State Agricultural Extension Advisory Services

1. RICE / PADDY CULTIVATION PRACTICES
Rice thrives in clay and clay loam soils with high water retention capability.
System of Rice Intensification (SRI) technique recommends transplanting 10-12 day old young single seedlings at wider spacing (25 cm x 25 cm).
Maintain 2-3 cm standing water during tillering and panicle initiation stages. Drain field 10 days before harvest to facilitate uniform ripening.

2. WHEAT SOWING & NPK MANAGEMENT
Optimal sowing window for wheat is November 1 to November 20. Sowing depth should be 4-5 cm using a seed drill.
Recommended seed rate is 40-45 kg per acre.
Apply balanced N:P:K at 50:25:25 kg/acre. Apply full Phosphorus and Potash with 50% Nitrogen at sowing; top dress remaining 50% Nitrogen during first irrigation at Crown Root Initiation (CRI) stage (21 days after sowing).
