================================================================================
TOMATO CULTIVATION & AGRONOMY ADVISORY GUIDE
Source Document: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) Agronomy Handbook
================================================================================

1. SOIL PREPARATION & PLANTING DEPTH
Tomato plants grow best in well-drained clay loam to sandy loam soils with a pH range of 6.0 to 7.0.
Land should be plowed 3-4 times to a fine tilth. Incorporate 8-10 tons of well-rotted farmyard manure (FYM) per acre during final plowing.
Seedlings should be transplanted at 25-30 days of age at a spacing of 60 cm x 45 cm. Plant seedlings to a depth of 5-7 cm to encourage strong adventitious root development.

2. IRRIGATION & MOISTURE MANAGEMENT
Tomato requires continuous, moderate soil moisture. Avoid alternate wetting and drying as it causes fruit cracking and blossom end rot.
Critical stages for irrigation are: immediately after transplanting, vegetative flowering, and fruit development.
Drip irrigation with fertigation is highly recommended, saving up to 40% water while increasing yield by 25%.

3. PEST & WEED MANAGEMENT
Major pests include Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera) and Whiteflies (Bemisia tabaci).
Install yellow sticky traps at 10 traps/acre to monitor whiteflies. For fruit borer, install pheromone traps at 5 traps/acre.
Keep the field weed-free for the first 45 days. Apply organic straw mulch (5-7 cm thickness) to suppress weeds and retain soil moisture.
